When Do Steelhead Run in Ontario? River-by-River Timing

Short answer

Steelhead follow the salmon in. On the Ganaraska and the other eastern tributaries the first fish arrive at the end of September. On the Credit, Humber and the rivers to the west it's October. Through the winter you can catch them sporadically on the reaches that stay open, and spring is good again around the trout opener at the end of April.

Updated October 6, 2026 · Based on on-river observation and the Ontario Fishing Regulations Summary 2026

The timing, river by river

Steelhead don't have a single run the way Chinook do. They come in behind the salmon in the fall, keep trickling in through the winter on warm rains and thaws, and push again in spring. The timing is similar from river to river. The east end of the lake starts a little earlier, and the bigger difference between rivers is which water is legal to fish once September 30 has passed.

Fall: steelhead follow the salmon

Steelhead come into the rivers behind the salmon and feed on their eggs, so the start of the steelhead season overlaps the end of the salmon run. On the Ganaraska and the other eastern tributaries that's the end of September. Further west, on the Credit, Humber and Bronte, it's October.

Late October and November are the best of the fall, while the water is still above about 5°C and the fish are fresh and willing to move to a bait. Like the salmon, they push upriver on rising water, so the day or two after a good rain is the time to go.

One November on the Credit I fished three spots before I found them. The first two gave me nothing. At the third I hooked two, lost one and landed one. If a spot has had a fair number of good drifts and nothing has happened, move.

Winter: fewer fish, deeper water

From December the fishing gets sporadic. Steelhead are still in the rivers and more come in on every warm rain or thaw, but they move less and take less. On January 1 the extended reaches close, so winter fishing happens on the all-year lower reaches in the first column of the table.

The most common mistake I see in winter is fishing too shallow and too fast. When the water is cold the fish are down deep and barely moving. Set the float deep enough that the bait drifts within about two feet of the bottom, hold it back so it moves slower than the surface, and give each piece of slow, deep water more drifts than you would in October. The winter steelhead guide covers where they sit as the water drops and the setup I use.

Spring: around the opener

Spring is the other good window. Fish that spent the winter in the rivers are joined by fresh ones from the lake, and they push upriver as the water warms. Around the trout opener at the end of April is a good time to be out.

Until the opener, only the all-year lower reaches are open. The opener is the fourth Saturday in April (April 24 in 2027), and that morning the extended reaches in the second column reopen. On the Credit that includes the Hwy 403 to Britannia stretch, which has been closed since August 15.

Check your exact reach

Boundaries are roads and rail lines, and a few hundred metres can be the difference between open water and a sanctuary. The two that catch people in the fall:

  • Credit River: Hwy 403 to the Britannia Rd bridge is closed August 15 to December 31.
  • Ganaraska River: Jocelyn St to the CNR bridge is closed September 1 to October 14, and above Jocelyn St to Hwy 401 is closed all year.

Frequently asked questions

When do steelhead run in Ontario?
Steelhead follow the salmon into Lake Ontario rivers. On the Ganaraska and other eastern tributaries the first fish arrive at the end of September; on the Credit, Humber and rivers to the west it is October. They can be caught sporadically through the winter on reaches that stay open, and spring is good again around the trout opener at the end of April.
When do steelhead run in the Credit River?
Steelhead come into the Credit in October, behind the Chinook, and November is a good month. Below Hwy 403 the river is open all year. Britannia Rd to Hwy 407 is open to December 31, but Hwy 403 to Britannia is a sanctuary from August 15 to December 31. In spring, fishing is good again around the late-April opener, when the water above Hwy 403 reopens.
When do steelhead run in the Ganaraska River?
The Ganaraska is one of the earliest steelhead rivers on Lake Ontario, with fish arriving at the end of September. Below the CNR bridge it is open all year. Jocelyn Street to the CNR is closed September 1 to October 14 and open again to December 31. Above Jocelyn Street, up to Hwy 401, is a sanctuary closed all year.
Can you catch steelhead in Ontario in winter?
Yes, sporadically, on the reaches that stay open after December 31. The most common mistake is fishing too shallow and too fast. In cold water steelhead sit deep and barely move, so set the float deep enough that the bait drifts close to the bottom and slow everything down.
When is the spring steelhead run in Ontario?
Spring is good around the trout opener, the fourth Saturday in April (April 24 in 2027). Until the opener only the all-year lower reaches are open. On the opener the extended reaches reopen, including the Hwy 403 to Britannia stretch of the Credit.
