Duffins Creek · Ajax and Pickering

Duffins Creek fishing regulations, and where to fish it

Duffins Creek reaches Lake Ontario in Ajax and gets runs of steelhead, Chinook and coho. Where you can fish depends on the date. From the lake to the CN railway line it is open all year. From the CN line to Highway 2 (Kingston Road) it is open until December 31. Above Highway 2, including Greenwood and Whitevale, it closes on September 30.

Before you go

Duffins Creek is in Zone 17. The two lower stretches come from the Durham Region exceptions in the regulations: open all year between the CN right-of-way and the lake, and to December 31 between Highway 2 and the CN right-of-way. Everything above Highway 2 follows the zone season.

Brook trout close on September 30 on every stretch. The exceptions don’t include them.

Daily limits with a Sport licence (Conservation licence in brackets): rainbow trout including steelhead 2 (1), brown trout 5 (2), Chinook and coho 5 (2), brook trout 2 (1). Trout and salmon together: 5 (2).

Boundaries are signed at the creek. Confirm the Zone 17 rules in the current Ontario fishing regulations before you fish.

Salmon usually peak from mid-September to mid-October, which overlaps the September 30 close above Highway 2. From October on, the runs are fished below Kingston Road.

Lower creek: open all year

Rotary Park and the marsh, Ajax

Rotary Park is at the mouth, off Lake Driveway West, with parking and the Waterfront Trail. The creek runs into Duffins Marsh before the lake, and fish gather here before they move upstream. It’s the busiest access during the fall salmon run and the spring steelhead run.

Up to the CN railway line

The creek between the marsh and the railway has pools and runs that suit a float. This is the stretch you can fish from January to April, so it gets most of the winter and early-spring steelhead pressure.

CN line to Kingston Road: open until December 31

From the railway up to Highway 2 the creek is open from the April opener through the fall, then closes on December 31. It gives you more water in October and November, when steelhead are moving up and the lower creek is crowded.

Above Highway 2: open until September 30

Greenwood Conservation Area

TRCA land in a deep valley north of Kingston Road. Salmon and steelhead pass through in season, but it closes on September 30, before the steelhead arrive.

Whitevale

The dam at Whitevale is the upper limit for migratory fish. Above it the creek holds resident trout. Check the signs at the dam before you fish near it.

Glen Major and the West Duffins

Small, cold tributaries on the Oak Ridges Moraine with brook trout and brown trout, open from the April opener to September 30. It’s redside dace habitat, so leave the baitfish alone.

Flow

The app reads the Water Survey of Canada gauge on Duffins Creek at Ajax (02HC049). On the lower creek it calls 2 to 5.5 m³/s prime, the flow a few days after rain, under 1 m³/s low, and about 13 m³/s or more blown out. Fresh fish move up on rising water after rain and are easiest to catch as it drops back.

Common questions

What are the fishing regulations on Duffins Creek?

Duffins Creek is in Zone 17. From Lake Ontario up to the CN railway line it is open all year for rainbow trout (steelhead), brown trout and salmon. From the CN line up to Highway 2 (Kingston Road) it is open from the fourth Saturday in April to December 31. Above Highway 2, including Greenwood and Whitevale, it is open from the fourth Saturday in April to September 30. Brook trout close on September 30 everywhere.

Can you fish Duffins Creek in the winter?

Yes, but only from Lake Ontario up to the CN railway line in Ajax, which is open all year. From January 1 to the fourth Saturday in April everything above the CN line is closed.

When do steelhead run in Duffins Creek?

The first steelhead come in from the lake in October, and more arrive through the fall. Some spend the winter in the lower creek, and the spring run follows from February into April.

When do salmon run in Duffins Creek?

Chinook and coho start entering the creek in late August and early September and peak from mid-September to mid-October. Above Highway 2 the season closes on September 30, so most of the run is fished below Kingston Road.

Can you fish at Greenwood Conservation Area or Whitevale?

Only from the fourth Saturday in April to September 30. Both are above Highway 2, so they are closed for the fall steelhead and late salmon. Whitevale Dam is the upper limit for migratory fish.
