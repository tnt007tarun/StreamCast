Technique & Gear

Primary method with recommended setup and baits. Tap alternatives to expand.

1
Hare's Ear Nymph
#12–16 · Natural / Gold bead
Universal confidence fly. Fish it anywhere, always.
2
Pheasant Tail Nymph
#14–18 · Copper wire rib
Deadly in clearer water or when fish are selective.
3
Tungsten Bead Caddis
#12–16 · Olive / Brown
Heavy bead gets to the bottom fast in higher flows.
4
Stonefly Nymph
#8–12 · Black / Brown, heavy
Big meal for big fish. Fish along the bottom in fast runs.
5
Zebra Midge
#18–22 · Black/silver or red/silver
When nothing else works in low clear water.
6
Sulphur Nymph
#14–16 · Yellow / Olive
Pre-hatch nymphing — fish the last hour before hatch begins.
1
Woolly Bugger
#4–8 · Olive, Black, Brown
Grand River standard. Olive in normal conditions, black in stained.
2
Sculpin Pattern
#4–6 · Tan / Brown, weighted
Browns eat sculpins year-round. Dead-drift then strip erratically.
3
Clouser Minnow
#4–8 · Chartreuse/White or Olive/White
Excellent in slightly elevated or stained water.
4
Articulated Streamer
#2–4 · Natural or White, jointed
Big fly for trophy fish. Strip fast, pause — erratic triggers reaction strikes.
1
Elk Hair Caddis
#14–16 · Tan / Olive
Evening staple. Caddis hatches reliable May–September.
2
Parachute Adams
#14–18 · Grey
All-purpose dry when you see rises but can't ID the hatch.
3
Sulphur Comparadun
#14–16 · Yellow / Orange
Match the evening sulphur hatch May–July. Low-riding profile.
4
Royal Wulff
#12–14 · Classic attractor
High-floating. Also works as a dry-dropper indicator.
1
Rapala Original Floating
4–7 cm · Firetiger, Perch, Rainbow
Classic crankbait for river browns. Perch in clear water, firetiger in stained.
2
Blue Fox Vibrax Spinner
#3–5 · Silver / Gold blade
Silver in clear conditions, gold in stained. Slow retrieve in cold.
3
Kastmaster Spoon
1/4–3/8 oz · Silver, Gold
Gets deep fast. Work it through pools and current seams near structure.
4
Mepps Aglia Spinner
#3–4 · Black/Yellow, Natural
Classic trout spinner. Consistent in all flows through seams.
1
Roe Bag
Small mesh sacs · Natural or cured
The most effective float fishing bait in Ontario rivers. Match size to flow — smaller in low clear water, larger in high stained water.
2
Soft Bead
10–12 mm · Pink, Peach, Natural
Highly effective for steelhead and salmon. Peg 1–2 inches above hook. Pink in coloured water, natural/peach in clear.
3
Hard Bead
8–12 mm · Nuke Egg colours
Peg 1–2 inches above hook. Chartreuse and orange are top producers.
4
Plastic Worm
3–4 inch · Red, Pink, Chartreuse
Thread onto hook and drift naturally. Red and pink for stained water, natural for clear.
5
Nightcrawler
Full or half · Natural
Classic bait for all trout species. Best in spring and after rain when naturals are washing in.
Bait rules vary by river — always verify current OMNR regulations before fishing.
