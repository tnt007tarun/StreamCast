From TJ

Hey guys, in November, the steelhead fishing’s going to pick up even more. I’ve already gotten into a couple and it’s only going to get better from here!

Also running

Coho are still in Bowmanville Creek and the Ganaraska, and lake-run browns take the same float and roe as steelhead.

Closed in November

Standard-season reaches, the Credit sanctuary (Hwy 403 to Britannia), Forks of the Credit, Lowville on Bronte, the Upper Grand, and the Ganaraska above Jocelyn St. Every river linked on this page is open all month. Brook trout are closed everywhere until April 24, 2027 (what stays open after September 30).

How we picked these dates

Open-Meteo forecasts for each river as of November 13, checked against four weather models, plus the app’s 7-day flow estimates from the Water Survey of Canada gauges. Each day is rated the same way as the app and the weekly email. Every river here is running below prime and clearing, and almost no rain is forecast until about Nov 22, so the cloudy days rate best. Nov 25 and 26 are 12 days out and the models disagree on a cold snap around then, so check the river in the conditions tool before you drive. This page is updated at the start of every month.
