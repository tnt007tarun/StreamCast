# HereFishyFishy: handoff as of 2026-10-06

Picks up from the 2026-10-04 handoff. Written at the end of a Claude Code
session with full read/write access to `tnt007tarun/StreamCast`. Everything
below is merged to `main` (PRs #51–#59) unless marked open.

## Architecture changes since 2026-10-04

**`app.css` now has four layers:**

```css
@layer tokens, app, patches, overrides;
```

* `tokens` (new, first): the design system. Palette, paper and panel surfaces,
  status colours in an on-paper and an on-panel version (`--good`/`--good-on-panel`
  etc.), font roles (`--font-serif/display/body/mono`), type scale (`--fs-*`),
  radii (`--r-*`), spacing (`--sp-*`), and `--content-max: 1140px` /
  `--band-pad` for the centred results column. **New rules read from these;
  don't write literal colours or sizes.** The old `:root` palettes moved here.
* `app` frozen, `patches` as before, `overrides` holds all new work.
* 10 real `!important` declarations remain (8 in `patches`, 2 in `overrides`).
  Each beats an inline `style` attribute set by JS. `assets/README.md` is current.

**Results page restyle: done** (was "abandoned twice"). Direction chosen from
three mockups: *editorial page, dark panel only for live data*.
* Page chrome, headings, cards: paper background, Charter serif headings.
* Only the hero condition tiles sit on the dark `--panel-bg`. The River
  Conditions cards were tried dark and moved back to light cards with a
  coloured top edge for status (user feedback: dark drew too much attention).
* Gear is a grid of "spec" tiles; top bait has a larger photo and Best for / How
  callouts.
* Every results section is centred on one 1140px column. Full-width bands keep
  edge-to-edge backgrounds and centre content with `--band-pad`.
* Inline styles on results markup were moved into classes. The season badge
  in `app.js` sets `data-state="good|warn|bad"` instead of hex colours.

## Logic fixes

* **Closed water is never recommended** (#53). `speciesFishableIn(section,
  species, date)` = species listed for the section AND `seasonClosureFor` says
  open AND species window is good/peak. A final pass in `buildTopPick` redirects
  to a sibling section holding the target species before offering a local
  substitute, and never offers a substitute on a closed stretch. The headline
  names the section as a link, with a "See <section> →" button that loads it
  (switching species if the suggestion is a different fish). `rankRivers`
  gives closed reaches −30 and a "Season closed on this reach" warning.
  Origin: Coho on the Upper Credit in October recommended Brown Trout on a
  stretch closed since Sept 30.
* **Stale "Closed on this date" notices** (#54). They were appended on every
  render and never removed, so Credit closures showed on the Humber. Now
  cleared per render and placed at the top of the access-card list (previously
  they landed in the cards/map grid and pushed the map down).
* "Out of season" label for pre-run Chinook/Coho/Steelhead had already been
  reworded on main ("not in rivers yet · may be staging near harbour mouths").
